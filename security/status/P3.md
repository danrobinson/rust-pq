## Proof structure

1. `g2Joint A` = joint sample `(pkSeed, tbl, (forgery, log))`; `g2_eq_joint`, `g2Class_eq_joint`
   express `g2` / `g2Class c` as `g2Joint A >>= pure ∘ b`; `prTrue_bind_pure` turns
   `prTrue (mx >>= pure ∘ b)` into `Pr{let z ← mx}[b z = true]`.
2. `stepSplit`: `prEvent_mono` + three `prEvent_or_le`, case split on the class.
3. `targetsOf pkSeed root log`: the ITSR target transcript as a *function of the G2 transcript*
   (one target per entry whose `(M, opt_rand)` did not occur earlier). `targetsOf_append`,
   `mem_targetsOf`.
4. `sim_step` / `sim_run`: running `bItsr`'s handler inside `ITSROracles` from state
   `targetsOf log` equals the `G2` handler, with the target state `targetsOf` of the new log
   (program equality, by induction on the forger).
5. `LogCovered`: every transcript entry's `(R, (PK.seed, PK.root, M))` is a target. It is a
   support invariant of `G2` (`g_step_covered`, `g_run_covered`, `g2Joint_covered`); it is what
   makes a *repeated* `(opt_rand, M)` harmless.
6. `bItsr_run`: `(bItsr A).run` = `G2` sample space mapped to `(candidate, targetsOf log)`.
7. `wins_of_itsr` (deterministic): fresh + class `itsr` + `LogCovered` ⇒ `ITSRProblem.Wins`.
8. `stepITSR`: `prEvent_mono_of_support`.
9. `bItsr_queryBound`: `IsQueryBoundP.simulateQ_run_add_inr_of_step` with per-step bounds
   `itsrRandomizer_bound`, `logSignOracle_bound`.

## Notes for dependants

- **`sampleTable` hazard (affects P0, P1, P4, P6).** Any tactic that gets a hypothesis or binder
  of the form `tbl ∈ support (sampleTable vp prims)` into play (`rcases`/`cases` on the result of
  `simp [mem_support_bind_iff]`, or `isQueryBoundP_bind`'s `∀ x ∈ support …` argument at
  `sampleTable`) made Lean loop at 100% CPU or hit `maximum recursion depth`, with no heartbeat
  error. Workaround used here: prove the step as a lemma over an *abstract* `my : ProbComp β`
  (`support_joint_aux`, `run_aux`, `bound_aux`) and instantiate it with `exact … (sampleTable vp
  prims) …`, giving the function arguments explicitly so unification is syntactic.
- Reusable, not ITSR-specific: `prTrue_bind_pure`, `g2Joint`, `g2_eq_joint`, `g2Class_eq_joint`,
  `support_joint_aux`. They live in `ITSR.lean`; other packages would have to copy them or the
  lead can move them to a shared file.
- `Mathlib`'s `add_le_add_right` in this toolchain adds on the *left*; use `add_le_add _ le_rfl`.
- The file sets `linter.unusedSectionVars false` (the section's `DecidableEq`/`Inhabited`
  variables are unused by most helper lemmas).
