## Notes
- `Structural.keyInj` is injectivity of `adrsToKey` on `{a | AddressFacts vp a}` only; packages
  needing distinct tweaks for addresses must supply `AddressFacts` (e.g. `addressFacts_*` lemmas in
  VCVio `EncodedTargets`). `KeySeparated` is NOT part of `Structural`; if a package needs it at
  128s, `keyDiscipline_128s.keySeparated` has it, but the generic theorem would need a new field.
- Non-vacuity does not say the assumptions hold for SHA-2; it says the games are not degenerate.
  With a degenerate `pkSeedDist` nothing changes in these lemmas (they hold for every `pkSeedDist`).
